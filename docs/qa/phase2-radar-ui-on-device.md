## Notes

A. Cold start splash: the manifest icon is `@android:drawable/ic_menu_search`, which Android 12+ draws as a grey magnifier on a large white disc in the middle of the dark splash. The window and bars are dark, so the check passes, but it is a bright element on first frame. Icon choice is the Director's (standing rule: approve icons first).

B. Stale rows lose their dimming after a process restart. Straight after a scan, hosts not found in that run (10.0.3.22, 10.0.3.50, STEVES-MSI-LAPT, 10.0.3.212) are drawn at 0.6 and read "not seen in the last scan"; after force-stop and relaunch the same rows are full strength (compare a05-settled-130 with a05-after-restart-130). `HomeViewModel.latestRun` starts at 0 and stale needs `run > 0`. Behaviour, not layout: open item, not fixed.

C. A scan cut short (network change or Stop) still shows "Scanned in N s" with the partial duration. The spec section 8 table has no stopped wording, so this needs a decision; open item, not fixed.

D. The Offline line ("Offline, showing cached results from <time>") only shows once a cache entry is past its TTL; a fresh entry is served from the cache before any network call. Reaching it would mean waiting out the TTL or editing app data, so it was not captured.

E. Settle overlap: for about 300 ms the fading-in headline and detail ("15 devices", "Scanned in 12 s...") are drawn across the shrinking ring because the text column is placed at its final spot while the ring is still moving left. It is brief and the text is still fading, but it reads as overlap when stepping frames. Director's eye.

F. With the segment fix the labels in the active yellow segment sit 2 dp from its edges at 1.3 ("Interesting"); readable, but tight. Director's eye.

G. The note field stays expanded and focused after Back hides the keyboard, and after tapping blank header space; it collapses when focus moves (for example tapping the list, pinned by `tappingTheListCollapsesTheExpandedNote`). Matches "losing focus collapses it"; recorded for the Director.

H. The instrumented suite runs a real discovery against the app's live database (after the second run Home read "16 devices, last scan 42 seconds ago"). Marks and notes were untouched (CHNAS still Interesting, note "QA note NAS").

Open items for the Director's eye (Radar spec 11): 11.1 arm in background, 11.2 chips collapse rule, 11.3 router teal, 11.4 four chosen edge colours and `border` strength, 11.5 teal switches, 11.6 section 12 items, plus the two deviations recorded in this plan (stale edge alpha 0.6, blip spiral).
